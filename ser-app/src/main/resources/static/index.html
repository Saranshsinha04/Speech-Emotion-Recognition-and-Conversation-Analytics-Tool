Responsible AI, Limitations & Go/No-Go Deployment Posture

  • Vocal Expression vs Internal State: Labels reflect perceived/acted vocal emotion in CREMA-D & RAVDESS, never mental health, personality, or true psychological state.
  • EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f): Workplace emotion recognition of employees/agents is prohibited in the EU; this product operates in customer-only mode by default.
  • Domain Shift & Telephony Robustness: Models trained on studio 16 kHz acted audio degrade under low SNR or 8 kHz telephony bandlimiting, and cross-corpus transfer (CREMA-D → RAVDESS) drops relative to in-corpus speaker-independent scores.
  • Subgroup Fairness & Calibration: Subgroup UAR across sex, age, and race/ethnicity plus temperature-scaled ECE are reported directly from test splits in the Research tab.
  • Go / No-Go Recommendation: Conditional Go for Assisted Post-Call Quality Triage (using calibrated HMM/Context Escalation flags as human-in-the-loop review ranking); No-Go for automated punitive action, real-time unsupervised decisions, or employee scoring.
